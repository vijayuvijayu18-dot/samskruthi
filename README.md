# Samskruthi
### Digital Heritage Preservation & Cultural Information Portal

Samskruthi is an open, global cultural repository dedicated to safeguarding and discovering the living heritage of humankind across all six continents:
- **Ancient Civilizations & Archaeological Discoveries** (Giza, Teotihuacan, Acropolis, Petra)
- **Monuments & World Architecture** (Machu Picchu, Angkor Wat, Venice Lagoon, Hampi)
- **Living Folk Arts & Craft Traditions** (Haida Totem Poles, Kyoto Woodblock, Wayang Kulit, Andalusian Flamenco)
- **Indigenous Heritage & Ecological Knowledge** (Andean Potato Seed Guardians, Australian Anangu Songlines)
- **Oral Histories & Living Memories** (Mali Griots, Elder Storytellers, Generational Ballads)
- **Historical Photographs & Visual Archives** (19th-century glass plates, field surveys)
- **Endangered Cultural Practices** (Disappearing dialects, sacred rituals, community crafts)
